Pick a road class, click to lay points, Enter or right-click to finish. Buildings snap to a road frontage when placed near one.
Pave a path turns a worn track into a road, keeping its wander.
Raise / Lower / Level reshape the ground — drag to sculpt. Water re-derives as you go, so dam a valley and a lake forms behind it.

Warbands — click a keep to muster, click a column to select it, click ground to march. Columns starve outside ground you have integrated, so a banner that has gone grey is a column dying. Turn Borders on to see the two pressures: a soft haze is culture, a hard bright line is soldiers, and hatching is ground you hold but have not made yours.
